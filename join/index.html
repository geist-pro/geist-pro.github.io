We are always looking for passionate researchers to join our group

We welcome applicants at all levels: PhD students, postdoctoral researchers, visiting scholars, and internship students. If none of the tracks fits, send a CV and one paragraph on what you want to build to the institutional e-mail address below.

How to apply

Who we are looking for

  1. 01

    Bachelor and Master theses

    Pick one of the open topics or propose your own in eyewear computing, cognitive augmentation, digitised emotions, human-robot interaction, participatory design in health care or serious games.

    What to send

    • a short exposé (problem, research question, method, preliminary outline)
    • transcript of records
    • one paragraph on why this topic
    Write to us about Bachelor and Master theses →
  2. 02

    PhD students

    Strong background in HCI, computer science, or related fields. Passion for hands-on prototyping and user studies.

    What to send

    • CV
    • publication list or thesis
    • one page on what you want to build
    Write to us about PhD students →
  3. 03

    Postdoctoral researchers

    Track record of first-author publications at top HCI venues. Interest in leading independent research threads.

    What to send

    • CV
    • publication list or thesis
    • one page on what you want to build
    Write to us about Postdoctoral researchers →
  4. 04

    Research interns and visiting scholars

    Undergraduate or Master's students with a concrete project idea or interest in one of our research areas.

    What to send

    • CV
    • transcript of records
    • one paragraph on your skills and availability
    Write to us about Research interns and visiting scholars →
  5. 05

    Student assistants (HiWi)

    Students of TU Clausthal can support running projects, from hardware prototyping to study support and software.

    What to send

Theses

Writing your thesis with us

All theses →

If you are nearing the end of your studies, planning to write your thesis soon, and have a passion for exploring the intersection of technology and human interaction, you’ve come to the right place. Our faculty is dedicated to the research and development of information systems that are not only technologically advanced but also intuitively tailored to people’s needs and behaviors. At a time when technology permeates every aspect of our lives, the importance of developing systems that are accessible, user-friendly, and effective cannot be overstated. Our department offers you the opportunity to immerse yourself in the research that will shape the future of human interaction with technology. Whether you are interested in improving usability, developing assistive technologies, or understanding the societal impacts of information systems, your thesis can make a significant contribution to this dynamic field.
