Augmented Humans 2020: taking a conference online

The co-organised Augmented Humans conference in Kaiserslautern moved to a fully virtual event because of COVID-19; Stelarc's keynote was pre-recorded and streamed.

EventPast

What
Augmented Humans 2020
Role
Organiser
When
16 to 17 Mar 2020
Where
Kaiserslautern, Germany (online)

On 16-17 March 2020, we co-organized the Augmented Humans conference in Kaiserslautern. Due to the circumstances related to COVID-19, we moved to a fully virtual event and cancelled the physical event on 13th March. The whole team was struggling and especially thanks to all of the chairs it came together quite well.

On Sunday, just we met (only 2 people, disinfection materials, social distancing and mask wearing in place) to pre-record Stelarc’s Keynote. The keynote was streamed on Monday morning. Stelarc was available during the streaming to answer questions in the chat and also took several questions after it over Zoom. I was surprised how well it worked. The chat worked as a perfect back channel, a lot of researchers engaged posed questions and provided interesting insights.

Source: kaikunze.de (16 March 2020).
