Keynote at Academic Mindtrek 2026

Kai Kunze gives the keynote "Augmenting Humans. From Physiology to Shared Experiences" at Academic Mindtrek 2026 in Tampere, Finland; the slides are online.

Kai Kunze gave the invited keynote at Academic Mindtrek 2026 in Tampere, Finland (27 to 30 October 2026) on 28 October. The keynote “Augmenting Humans. From Physiology to Shared Experiences” looks at designing feedback loops in the age of AI: from activity recognition and wearable sensing to shared physiological experiences on stage.

The slides are available as a PDF: Mindtrek-Keynote-Kai-2026.pdf (9 MB).
