Dominik Schulz

Health and care, serious games, extended reality

In the group since 2024

  • Email→
  • Institute page→

Dominik Schulz trained as an IT specialist in application development, studied Computer Science (B.Sc.) at TU Clausthal and Digital Technologies (M.Sc., Ostfalia / TU Clausthal), and has been a research associate at the chair since March 2024, where he teaches, supervises theses and publishes. He co-developed CLARA, an AI-based voice documentation system for nursing care, and also works as a developer in a start-up.
