Prof. Dr. Kai Kunze has held the W3 professorship for Human-Centered Information Systems at the Institut für Informatik of TU Clausthal since 2026. Before his appointment he was Professor at the Graduate School of Media Design at Keio University in Yokohama, Japan, and earlier Assistant Professor at Osaka Prefecture University; he received his PhD from the University of Passau. He is known for his work on eyewear computing and on activity recognition that stays robust across sensor placements.

Projects

All research →

  • Active

    A Placebo Concert

    Telling people that abstract visuals represent audience physiology changes a VR concert more than whether the data is real (CHI '25).

  • Active

    Abstraction Beats Realism

    Abstract visualisations of audience arousal synchronise VR concert visitors with the original live audience better than 360-degree video (AHs 2026).

  • Active

    Augmented Perception

    Smart glasses and wearables as a sensing and actuation platform that sits directly at the human senses.

  • Active

    Cognitive Augmentation

    Wearable sensing that captures reading, attention and gaze synchrony, applied to cognitive health and dementia prevention.

  • Active

    Cuddle Fish

    A soft floating robot with flapping wings that people can safely touch, pat and hug (AHs '25).

  • Active

    Digitalized Emotion

    Making emotional states measurable, transferable and shareable, from audience physiology at live performances to digital empathy.
