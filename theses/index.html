Theses

6 open topics for Bachelor and Master theses. Own proposals in these fields are welcome; every thesis starts with a short exposé.

How to write the exposéGet in touch

6 topics

Open topics

  1. Open topic

    Cognitive augmentation

    Kognitive Augmentierung

    Research areaCognitive Augmentation→

    Reading and attention recognition, gaze synchronisation and activity recognition; applications for cognitive health up to dementia prevention.

    Prerequisites

    Basic machine learning and a user-study mindset.

    Contact
    Prof. Dr. Kai KunzeProfessor and head of the chair
    Topic and how to apply: Cognitive augmentation→

  2. Open topic

    Digitalised emotions

    Digitalisierte Emotionen

    Research areaDigitalized Emotion→

    Capturing, transmitting and sharing emotional reactions, e.g. audience physiology at live performances (BoilingMind, Frisson Waves) or systems for digital empathy and sustainable behaviour.

    Prerequisites

    Interest in physiological sensing and study design.

    Contact
    Prof. Dr. Kai KunzeProfessor and head of the chair
    Topic and how to apply: Digitalised emotions→

  3. Open topic

    Eyewear computing and wearable sensing

    Eyewear Computing und tragbare Sensorik

    Research areaAugmented Perception→

    Smart glasses and wearables that unobtrusively capture electrodermal activity, eye movements (EOG) and body motion in everyday life; from hardware prototype to signal analysis.

    Prerequisites

    Interest in hardware prototyping or signal processing; Python helps.

    Contact
    Prof. Dr. Kai KunzeProfessor and head of the chair
    Topic and how to apply: Eyewear computing and wearable sensing→

  4. Open topic

    Human-robot interaction (HRI)

    Mensch-Roboter-Interaktion (HRI)

    Research areaFloating Companion→

    Design and evaluation of interaction between people and robots, e.g. with soft floating robots following the DIS '26 paper “Floating Companion”, or in the context of the Human-AI and Human-Robot Cooperation lab.

    Prerequisites

    Prototyping skills (hardware or software) and interest in user studies.

    Contact
    Prof. Dr. Kai KunzeProfessor and head of the chair
    Topic and how to apply: Human-robot interaction (HRI)→

  5. Open topic

    Serious games and human-machine interaction

    Serious Games und Mensch-Maschine-Interaktion

    Research areaCognitive Augmentation→

    Game-based applications and user-centred interaction design, building on the chair's courses.

    Prerequisites

    Game or interface development experience from the chair's courses.

    Contact
    Prof. Dr. Kai KunzeProfessor and head of the chair
    Topic and how to apply: Serious games and human-machine interaction→

  6. Open topic

    Participatory design in health care

    Partizipatives Design im Gesundheitswesen

    Research areaCognitive Augmentation→

    Designing digital tools for care and health settings together with the people who use them, e.g. the further development of CLARA, the chair's speech documentation system for care: offline speech recognition, multilingualism, integration with existing documentation systems.

    Prerequisites

    Software engineering; interest in participatory methods, speech recognition or healthcare IT.

    Contact
    Dominik SchulzResearch associate
    Topic and how to apply: Participatory design in health care→
