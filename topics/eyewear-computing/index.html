Research topic

Smart eyewear and eye tracking

Glasses that sense gaze, blinks and facial muscles to read attention and reading behaviour.

Projects · 4Publications · 14Theses · 1People · 6

Research · 4

Projects

  • ActiveKeio · TU Clausthal

    Augmented Perception

    Smart glasses and wearables as a sensing and actuation platform that sits directly at the human senses.

    GEIST lab

  • ActiveKeio · TU Clausthal

    Cognitive Augmentation

    Wearable sensing that captures reading, attention and gaze synchrony, applied to cognitive health and dementia prevention.

    Kai Kunze

  • ArchiveKeio · GEIST

    Eyewear Computing

    Smart glasses as cognitive assistants: sensing cognitive states and cognition-aware interaction, from Google Glass and J!NS MEME to the Apple Vision Pro.

    Kai Kunze

  • ArchiveKeio · GEIST

    Open Eyewear

    An open eyewear platform to quantify social and cognitive functions in real life and to design interventions that improve behaviour (JST PRESTO).

    GEIST lab
